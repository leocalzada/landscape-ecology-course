Through the animal's eyes

This site is your game master. It shows only what your animal can see and rolls the die. Draw everything you see on your paper grid.

Play with these questions in mind

  1. What does my animal need, and can it see any of it from here?
  2. When home is out of sight, how do we choose a direction?
  3. Is a short cut across a road worth the risk?
  4. Is there less forest here, or is it just broken into small pieces? Does that matter to my animal?
  5. Is my animal picky (a specialist) or happy almost anywhere (a generalist)?
